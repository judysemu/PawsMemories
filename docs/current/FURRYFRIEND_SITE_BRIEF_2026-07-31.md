# FurryFriend.cc Site and Editorial Cascade Brief — 2026-07-31

## Purpose

Build `furryfriend.cc` as a genuinely useful pet-keepsake education site that
is clearly presented as **FurryFriend, a Pawsome3D guide**. It should answer
real customer questions, help people make good decisions, and send a reader to
Pawsome3D only when the product is relevant. It must not be a thin keyword
network, a duplicate of Pawsome3D, or an auto-publishing content farm.

Primary conversion links:

- Create a keepsake: `https://pawsome3d.com/create`
- See pricing: `https://pawsome3d.com/pricing`
- Learn how it works: `https://pawsome3d.com/how-it-works`

Do not publish exact delivery times, prices, print materials, warranties, or
model-quality promises unless they are verified against the live Pawsome3D
product. Until that production review is complete, use careful language such
as “check current options on Pawsome3D.”

## Brand system

Mirror Pawsome3D's warm, reassuring TerraPaw visual language without making
FurryFriend indistinguishable from the product app:

- primary brown `#442a22`; primary container `#5d4037`;
- warm peach `#e7bdb1` and pale peach `#ffdbd0`;
- slate blue `#4c616c` and pale blue `#cfe6f2`;
- warm surface `#faf9f5`, white cards, dark text `#1a1c1a`;
- rounded cards, soft shadows, generous whitespace, calm editorial layouts;
- Plus Jakarta Sans headings, Be Vietnam Pro body, Space Grotesk labels;
- use the Pawsome3D logo only with a clear “by Pawsome3D” relationship;
- accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive mobile
  layout, reduced-motion support, and descriptive image alternatives.

Voice: kind, clear, practical, emotionally aware, and never saccharine. Lead
with the answer. Explain technical terms in ordinary pet-owner language. Treat
memorial topics sensitively and do not give veterinary, mental-health, legal,
or financial advice.

## Public information architecture

1. **Home** — “Helpful answers for keeping your pet's story close.” Search or
   browse by question; feature photo-prep, 3D keepsakes, memorials, printing,
   digital models, gifts, privacy, and troubleshooting.
2. **Guides hub** — filters by topic and intent, with human-readable cards and
   no empty tag pages.
3. **Article pages** — direct answer, key takeaways, step-by-step guidance,
   decision checklist, source notes, author/reviewer, published/updated dates,
   related guides, and one context-appropriate Pawsome3D action.
4. **Ask FurryFriend** — question intake that searches existing answers first;
   unanswered questions become editorial candidates, not instant public AI
   pages.
5. **About and editorial policy** — ownership, mission, author/reviewer
   identity, how AI assists, source standards, correction policy, and contact.
6. **Privacy and terms** — plain-language handling of submitted questions; do
   not collect pet photos in the guide site unless a separately reviewed,
   necessary feature is added.
7. **Admin editorial desk** — authenticated only; queues, evidence ledger,
   draft review, approve/reject, revision history, refresh due date, and
   publication controls.

## Required editorial pillars

### 1. Pet memorials and keepsakes

Lead with **“How to Honor a Beloved Pet's Memory.”** Build a sensitive series
about choosing photos, preserving distinctive details, involving family,
creating a remembrance space, and deciding between digital and physical
keepsakes. Position Pawsome3D custom 3D models and Pawprints as the hero modern,
tactile options for celebrating a pet's life. The comparison must explain who
each option suits and may use “ultimate” as an editorial theme only when the
body substantiates the choice rather than stating an unverifiable universal
claim.

### 2. Tech-savvy pet parents

Lead with **“How to Turn a Single Photo Into an Animated 3D Video.”** Explain
the customer journey in plain language: smartphone photo selection, reference
creation, 3D build, optional rigging, scene/animation work, review, and export.
Use “ears lift, head turns, and fur catches the light” as the desired visual
story, not as a guarantee for every source image. Publish the product-specific
version only after a live Pawsome3D animation run proves the exact steps and
outcome; otherwise label the piece as a concept/behind-the-scenes guide and
remove unsupported product promises.

### 3. Unique pet gifts

Lead with **“10 Unique Gifts for Dog Owners Who Have Everything.”** Make the
list genuinely comparative and useful: recipient fit, emotional tone,
personalization input, planning effort, digital versus physical delivery, and
what to verify before buying. Feature **Send a Pawprint** prominently once the
pre-paid gift entitlement is implemented and live-tested. Until then, describe
only the currently verified digital Pawprint email-send behavior.

### 4. Historical and fun avatars

Lead with **“What Your Cat Would Look Like as a Pre-1900 Historical Figure.”**
Use a highly visual, playful editorial format built around role-based concepts
such as The Composer, The Naturalist, The Novelist, and The Nightingale. Keep
subjects pre-1900 and role-described unless a separate rights review approves
specific identities. Link to an actual limited-edition collection only after
the collection has real images, SKUs/offer records, availability, and working
destinations; never present placeholder glyphs or generic Pawprints navigation
as a purchasable collection.

## Product-truth gates discovered during the source review

These are hard launch gates, not copy-edit suggestions:

- The current Pawsome3D home card advertises “45% off their 3D model” and “you
  get the credit,” but the current Send a Pawprint endpoint only emails an
  already-created Pawprint image and its normal creation price. It has no
  pre-paid gift purchase, recipient entitlement, discount, or sender reward.
- The current historical collection card contains four role glyphs and three
  hard-coded merch prices, but every action routes to the generic Pawprints
  studio. There are no collection-specific product records or destinations in
  that card.
- The current one-photo animation card promises a hyper-real video with
  specific ear/head/fur motion, while the Animation Studio is release-gated and
  the required live provider path has not yet passed this production review.

FurryFriend must not publish these as available product facts until the
corresponding Pawsome3D repair and live acceptance is recorded. The editorial
desk should visibly flag each claim as `blocked_unverified_product_claim` in
the evidence ledger.

## SALTI-style editorial cascade

Every generated draft moves through a durable, inspectable state machine:

`question_received → intent_qualified → sources_collected → evidence_checked →
answer_planned → drafted → seo_reviewed → fact_safety_reviewed →
human_review_required → published → monitored`

Roles:

1. **Question and intent agent** — clusters the exact customer problem,
   audience, journey stage, sensitivity, and search phrasing; rejects off-topic
   or unsafe requests.
2. **Evidence agent** — collects current first-party Pawsome3D facts and
   authoritative external sources; records URL, title, retrieval date, claim,
   and confidence. It may not draft unsupported claims.
3. **Answer architect** — creates an answer-first outline, decision logic,
   practical checklist, and explicit unknowns.
4. **Product-fit agent** — determines whether Pawsome3D is genuinely relevant,
   picks at most one primary action, and blocks unsupported commercial claims.
5. **Writer agent** — drafts in the FurryFriend voice from the approved
   evidence and outline; it cannot add facts absent from the ledger.
6. **Search and structure agent** — produces a descriptive title, unique meta
   description, concise slug, semantic headings, internal links, image plan,
   and valid Article/Breadcrumb JSON-LD. It does not force keywords.
7. **Fact, safety, and originality gate** — checks every factual/product claim,
   citation coverage, duplicate/thin content, grief sensitivity, misleading
   functionality, and accidental professional advice. Any failure returns the
   draft to the correct earlier stage.
8. **Human publish gate** — the only role allowed to publish. It records the
   reviewer, approval time, version, and disposition of warnings.
9. **Refresh monitor** — flags stale product claims, broken links, declining
   usefulness, and customer corrections; it never changes a public page without
   human review.

Required durable records: `questions`, `article_jobs`, `source_evidence`,
`article_versions`, `quality_checks`, `publication_approvals`, and
`content_metrics`. Every stage is idempotent and version-bound. Failed jobs
remain visible and retryable; they cannot silently publish partial output.

If a native server-side AI runner is unavailable, ship the cascade as an
honest draft/review workflow with the eight seed articles. Show “AI drafting is
not configured” in admin; never simulate completed agent work in the public UI.
Do not expose model credentials in browser code.

## Search and technical requirements

- Server-render or pre-render public article content so useful text, title,
  description, canonical, author, and dates are present without requiring a
  client-side interaction.
- One stable, self-referential canonical URL per article. Do not duplicate
  Pawsome3D's pages or create city/breed/template permutations.
- Generate a root XML sitemap containing only canonical, indexable pages;
  reference it from `robots.txt`.
- Add accurate Organization/WebSite on the home page and Article plus
  BreadcrumbList on articles. Do not promise FAQ rich-result eligibility.
- Include visible author and reviewer pages, source links, AI-assistance
  disclosure, date published, date modified, and correction route.
- Use descriptive, unique titles and meta descriptions; proper heading order;
  internal links based on reader needs; Open Graph/Twitter metadata; and large,
  original or properly licensed images with provenance.
- Provide a friendly 404, secure admin routes, validated inputs, rate limits on
  question submission, privacy-preserving analytics, no trackers by default,
  and no public secrets.
- Include an admin launch checklist for Google Search Console ownership,
  sitemap submission, URL inspection, rich-result validation, indexing checks,
  and performance monitoring. Do not claim indexing or ranking until observed.

## Launch acceptance

- `https://furryfriend.cc/`, all eight article URLs, About, Editorial Policy,
  Privacy, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` return the correct status and content.
- No broken links, missing images, console errors, mixed content, accidental
  noindex, or non-canonical duplicates.
- Structured data validates and exactly matches visible page content.
- Every product assertion is either verified against live Pawsome3D or removed.
- A submitted question creates one durable editorial candidate, is rate-limited,
  and does not become public without recorded human approval.
- Mobile, keyboard, contrast, reduced-motion, and performance checks pass.
- Domain mapping and TLS for `furryfriend.cc` are verified independently after
  the generated Hostinger project is ready.

## Policy basis checked on 2026-07-31

This plan follows current Google Search Central guidance: content should be
people-first and demonstrate clear authorship and process; scaled low-value AI
pages are spam regardless of how they are produced; Article and Breadcrumb
structured data must match visible content; canonical and sitemap URLs should
be consistent; and FAQ rich results are generally limited to authoritative
government and health sites. Search visibility is a monitored outcome, not a
guarantee.
